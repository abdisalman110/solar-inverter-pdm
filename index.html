Four-Layer Predictive Maintenance Architecture

1. Physical Model (Fast)

Simulink 200 W PV Array → Boost (20 kHz, P&O MPPT) → 48 V DC-Link (6.8 mF + ESR) → Full-Bridge Inverter (10 kHz Unipolar SPWM) → LC Filter → 24 Vrms 50 Hz Grid + Foster RC thermal networks.

2. Ageing & Features (Slow)

Two-time-scale MATLAB loop: Arrhenius 10 °C rule for capacitor + Coffin-Manson & Miner's rule for IGBT. Online 100 Hz FFT extracts Z(2f) → ESRest, Cest with realistic sensor noise.

3. AI Analytics (Python)

Unit-level 70/15/15 split (zero leakage). Autoencoder vs. IsoForest/OCSVM anomaly detection; RF/SVM/MLP 4-class health classification (GroupKFold); LSTM/GRU sliding-window Capacitor RUL.

4. Decision Support

Schedules preventive capacitor replacement when predicted RUL ≤ 2,500 h, balancing avoided breakdown costs against remaining useful capacitor life.

Honest Project Scope & Limitations

  • Simulation-Only Study: All waveforms and degradation trajectories come from our MATLAB/Simulink physical model; no real outdoor field inverter data was collected.
  • Illustrative, Time-Accelerated Constants: Arrhenius L0 and Coffin-Manson A constants are scaled so units reach EOL cleanly within ~40 snapshots.
  • Two Fault Mechanisms Only: Models DC-link capacitor wear-out (main RUL target) and IGBT bond-wire/solder fatigue (assumed equal across all 4 H-bridge IGBTs); excludes short/open-circuit or grid faults.
  • Zero Reused Paper Metrics: Follows the four-layer architecture of Karim (TechRxiv, Oct 2025) without reusing any of its unverified accuracy figures.
